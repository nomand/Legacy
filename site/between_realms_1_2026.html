Between Realms 1 2026

Digital  ‣  Projection Mapping  ‣  Between Realms 1 2026

    Between Realms Part 1 was the first installment of an immersive experience that brought the magic of Mystic Realms festival to the Button Factory, a creative venue in central Auckland.

    We had two stages going across two floors. I was responsible for the digital pre-production and live performance with lighting and projection mapping visuals during the event.

    The mainstage was constructed out of kit pieces designed for Mystic Realms and is the first time we used a two-projector setup.
