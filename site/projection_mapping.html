Quarry Arts Festival Of Light 2026

I had the pleasure of lighting up Studio 21 at 2026 Whangarei Quarry Arts Festival of Light, a 4 day family friendly night of light and magic. Over the course of 4 days, projection artists, interactive light installation artists, UV & fire performers and a wonderful crew have lit up the old quarry for the local community to experience.

Kermesse

Kermesse was a special party by Call of The Tribe and Audioart, celebrating Laulau's birthday and completion of her adventure in NZ.

I was performing live visuals alongside Zyness B2B EDZ, Full AUM, Zagada and Laulau at Casette9 in Auckland CBD.

Mystic Realms 2026

Mystic Realms 2026 was a very special festival. Elven Realm was a stage design co-created with a fellow artist Marina

I was the digital backbone of the production with many roles in the process, spanning manufacturing planning, technical design, engineering, CNC file prep, projection mapping production & live performance.

The entire experience was made possible thanks to a huge effort by many fantastic volunteers, creatives, builders, painters, decorators, lighting crew, kitchen, sound and many more.

Between Realms 1 2026

Between Realms Part 1 was the first installment of an immersive experience that brought the magic of Mystic Realms festival to the Button Factory, a creative venue in central Auckland.

We had two stages going across two floors. I was responsible for the digital pre-production and live performance with lighting and projection mapping visuals during the event.

The mainstage was constructed out of kit pieces designed for Mystic Realms and is the first time we used a two-projector setup.

Dimension 2025

There's a DJ for Everyone was an AI animation experiment.

It utilized Stable Diffusion, IPAdapters, AnimateDiff models along with various upscalers and interpolators to achieve a set of psychedelic morphing loops that explore a variety of visual and music styles.

In 2025, I exhibited a 25 minute version of this work at Dimension Festival in 2025. It seamlessly morphed between 50 different characters.

Some of the individual animations can be seen on the project TikTok
