Irie is a 33ft steel sailboat that for 3 years was my home, my workspace and playground. It was the biggest object I used to own and marked an era of massive challenge and adventure.

In 2021 I bought Irie with intention to move aboard and forego the common formula for life and take on a cruising lifestyle.
In 2022 I moved aboard with my then partner.
In 2023 Irie was hauled out for a major refit aacross all systems.
Since, life changed fast and in a bid to remain flexible, mobile and true to my circumstance, I decided to step away.
In 2026 I moved on from Irie.

A digital tribute and tombstone can be viewed at [Dear Irie](https://dearirie.com)
