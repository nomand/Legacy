**The Siren’s Lair** was a DNB/Techno/PSYTech stage at Octoblast 2026. A combined effort of exceptional people coming together to create a space that moves and breathes, a space with layers, that’s alive day and night, a vessel, a stage production and an audiovisual spectacle.

This was the 4th installment of our stage design kit, since it's first debut as Elven Realm at [Mystic Realms](mystic_realms_2026)

This was a very special installation, bringing together lessons and improvements attained running [Between Realms 1](between_realms_1_2026) and [Between Realms 2](between_realms_2_2026) events.

Putting this stage experience together marked a significant improvement in team organization, planning and process, structure scale, technological sophistication
