Fluff'N'Kuffs was a fundraser event for the Blunderland Kiwiburn camp (formerly Nocturnal Wonderland).
Held at KUFFS, a unique kink venue in Auckland CBD.

The venue offered challenging space for projection mapping and performance, from a fairly confined jail cell.
Performing alongside the DJ lineup, I drove the visuals, projecting onto the jail bars and the artwork behind us.
