I had the pleasure of lighting up Studio 21 at 2026 Whangarei Quarry Arts Festival of Light, a 4 day family friendly night of light and magic.
Over the course of 4 days, projection artists, interactive light installation artists, UV & fire performers and a wonderful crew have lit up the old quarry for the local community to experience.
