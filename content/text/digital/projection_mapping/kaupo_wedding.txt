I had the privilege of mapping a very unique venue for a wedding of dear friend of mine.

A former chapel with two massive white barn doors served as a spectacular projection surface. Barn doors ajar, a beautiful stained glass window was the highlight behind the DJ.

The stained glass pattern became the centerpiece of a psychedelic triptych, honoring the former place of worship and connection with the divine.
