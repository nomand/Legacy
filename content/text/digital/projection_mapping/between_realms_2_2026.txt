Between Realms Part 2 was the second installment of an immersive experience that brought the magic of Mystic Realms festival to the Button Factory, a creative venue in central Auckland.

The mainstage was constructed out of kit pieces designed for [Mystic Realms](mystic_realms_2026) with addition of the octopus, designed by [Tony Ellia](https://www.instagram.com/toni_ellia)

This setup marked a transition in my visual performances away from scene-driven architecture to midi effect-driven control, leaning even more into live performance and empowering my visual sets with more tactile control.
